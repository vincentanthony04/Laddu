# Project Laddu — Level-5 Product Contract

Level-5 is an evidence standard, not a self-awarded score. Required controls include immutable candidate/population identity, point-in-time feature evidence, forward-only governance, Signal Age, continuous reassessment, append-only lifecycle evidence, venue-correct post-cost economics, fold-local walk-forward proof, capital-constrained simulation, forward Model Paper observation, durable settlement/learning lineage, explicit failure recovery, and consistent authority IDs across the operator UI.

No model, learning loop, source test, backtest, or installer result can grant broker authority. Material model/rule changes remain governed and fail closed. Model influence remains zero until the required evidence gates pass, and any governed non-zero influence remains capped by the release identity.


R18 complexity contribution: a Hybrid/ML challenger can progress to isolated challenger review only after it beats the deterministic mathematical baseline on matched frozen populations with a positive 95% moving-block-bootstrap lower bound, positive population-level consistency, and regime robustness. Null-alpha falsification and leakage-canary proof are mandatory. Matured efficacy failure removes ML ranking authority while preserving the mathematical baseline. Active shadow challengers can be escalated to human quarantine review from forward evidence; automatic production mutation remains prohibited.

R20 retained-data utilization: existing canonical history must participate before scarce scanner deep-analysis capacity is assigned. A local-history scheduling prior may reorder analysis priority only; it cannot remove canonical universe members, create Evidence Engine conviction, bypass risk/admission gates, or trigger provider history I/O. Cross-sectional research freshness is per-symbol and accumulates across immutable-universe batches. Standard Delivery ML walk-forward must consume every eligible chronological fold after a multi-year initial training window when retained PIT history permits; bounded first-use mode remains shadow-only and cannot satisfy Level-5 evidence by itself.

R21 evidence closure: genuine forward evidence is cumulative and stratified into 126/252/504/756-trading-day durability tiers with increasing settled-candidate, population and regime requirements; historical replay never counts as elapsed forward time. Historical capital validation accepts only fold-local models, immutable historical OOF model lineage, or genuinely prospective immutable predictions, and capital proof additionally requires mark-to-market and no-leverage evidence. Governance PostgreSQL selector evidence is the primary replay authority after verified migration. During market hours production acceptance also requires both Intraday and Delivery scanners to prove quote-ready deep mathematical analysis; a healthy API shell, warming state, blank scanner, or unexplained zero-candidate surface is not sufficient.

R21 final-market closure additionally binds source attestation by recomputing the sealed source tree during deployment validation. Production proof is symmetric across desks: market-hours scanner acceptance requires real quote-ready deep mathematics for both Intraday and Delivery, Intraday must independently pass capital-profile selector replay on immutable same-population evidence, and Delivery must independently pass historical OOF capital WFA. A rendered UI/API shell or blank/warming scanner can never satisfy this gate.
